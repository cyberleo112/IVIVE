Assumptions

  • The app's dog GastroPlus defaults were used: 10 kg body weight, 18.54 L/h liver blood flow, 324.5 g liver weight, and 120 million hepatocytes/g liver.
  • IV 1 mg/kg was converted to 10 mg total dose for the 10 kg default dog.
  • Concentration units were treated as ng/mL, equivalent to ug/L. The NCA dose was therefore entered as 10000 ug.
  • Terminal lambda_z used the 8, 24, and 48 h points.
  • MRT and Vss use IV bolus NCA first-moment equations: AUMC extrapolated to infinity, MRTinf_obs = AUMCinf_obs/AUCinf_obs, and Vss_obs = CL x MRTinf_obs.
  • No independent literature-reported observed clearance was provided for this ad hoc case.
